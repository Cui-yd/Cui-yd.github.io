Welcome to my homepage!         

I am currently a [CANSSI postdoctoral fellow](https://canssi.ca/story/cdpf-yidan-cui/) working with [Dr. Dehan Kong](https://sites.google.com/site/kongdehanstat/) from the University of Toronto and [Dr. Qihuang Zhang](https://qihuangzhang.github.io/index) from the McGill University. Before that, I received my PhD in Biostatistics from Shanghai Jiao Tong University, under the supervision of [Dr. Zhangsheng Yu](https://life.sjtu.edu.cn/teacher/en/ZhangshengYu).        

My research develops statistical methods for high-dimensional omics data and complex phenotypes. Currently, I focus on multi-slice spatial transcriptomics, building methods to register tissue sections into 3D reference atlases. During my PhD, I developed methods for rare-variant association testing in whole-genome sequencing studies and for high-dimensional mediation analysis with DNA methylation data.        

Outside of research, I enjoy hiking and swimming, and I’m always looking for a new trail to explore.        

Feel free to reach out about research, collaboration, or trail recommendations!
